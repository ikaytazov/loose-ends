How it works

Your team stays in email. You see the whole picture.

Nobody has to open another tool. Loose Ends does the chasing.

  1. 1Paste the notesLoose Ends proposes each task with its owner, date and the exact sentence it came from. You approve.
  2. 2A short morning emailAt 8 a.m. everyone gets only their own tasks, most urgent first.
  3. 3They just reply“Done.” “Blocked.” “Can I have until Friday?” The board updates itself.

The key moment

One plain email. Several correct changes.

A reply can report progress, flag a blocker and ask for more time at once. The date only moves when the owner says yes.

Why teams trust it

The AI suggests. People stay in charge.

Dates never move from a replyA request for more time goes to the workstream owner. Nothing changes until they approve.
Every change shows its sourceEach update quotes the exact words from the meeting or the email that caused it.
Unclear means a questionIf a reply could mean two things, Loose Ends asks instead of guessing. No reply shows as unknown, never as on track.
